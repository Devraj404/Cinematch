The Science
THREE ALGORITHMS
01 / CONTENT-BASED
What You Like
Analyzes the genres, descriptions, and metadata of movies you've liked. Uses TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity to find movies that are textually and structurally similar to your favorites. Works great for niche preferences.
02 / COLLABORATIVE
Who You're Like
Finds users with similar rating patterns to yours, then recommends what they loved that you haven't seen. Uses a user-item matrix and K-nearest neighbors. Powers recommendations on Netflix and Amazon.
03 / HYBRID
Best of Both
Combines both approaches with a weighted score (α·CF + (1-α)·CB). Handles the cold-start problem for new users, and consistently outperforms either method alone. This is the production algorithm.
